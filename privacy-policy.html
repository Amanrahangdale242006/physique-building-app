Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2026

1. Introduction

AuraSculpt AI ("we", "our", or "the App") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information when you use our mobile application and web platform.

2. Data Collection & Local Processing

AuraSculpt AI is engineered with a strict Privacy-First Architecture:

  • Camera & Photo Access: Meal photos and physique snapshots are captured solely for optical nutritional evaluation and calorie estimation.
  • Local Storage: Your biometric metrics, target macros, workout logs, and league rank points are stored locally on your device via HTML5 LocalStorage and IndexedDB.
  • No Unauthorized Data Brokerage: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal health data to third-party advertising networks.

3. Permissions Requested

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used exclusively to scan meals when you activate the meal photo scanner.
  • Storage / Media: Used solely to allow you to select existing meal photos from your device library.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is not targeted towards individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. You are advised to review this page periodically for any modifications.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach us via our support repository or email support@aurasculpt.app.

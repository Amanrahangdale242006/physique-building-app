Publish to Google Play Store & Deploy Live

Launch AuraSculpt AI publicly on the web and packaging for Android Play Store

METHOD 1: INSTANT INSTALL (RECOMMENDED)

📲 Install as Native Android App (Without waiting for Play Store Review)

Your app is already configured as a fully compliant Progressive Web App (PWA) with an offline Service Worker and Play Store Manifest.

  1. Open this app on your Android phone's Google Chrome.
  2. Tap the "📲 Install App" button in the top bar (or tap Chrome's 3-dot menu ⋮ > "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen").
  3. AuraSculpt AI installs directly onto your phone screen with its own app icon, splash screen, and full native screen access!
METHOD 2: PUBLIC CLOUD DEPLOYMENT (FREE & PERMANENT)

🌐 Deploy to Free Public HTTPS (GitHub Pages or Vercel)

Google Play Store requires your app to have a live public HTTPS address. You can deploy this exact folder in 60 seconds:

Option A: Vercel / Netlify (Fastest)
  1. Go to vercel.com or netlify.com
  2. Drag & drop this project folder (`new project'`) onto their dashboard.
  3. Your app goes live instantly at https://aurasculpt.vercel.app with free automatic HTTPS!
Option B: GitHub Pages
  1. Push this folder to a GitHub repository.
  2. Go to Settings > Pages > select main branch > Save.
  3. Your app is hosted live at https://<username>.github.io/<repo>/.
STEP 3: OFFICIAL GOOGLE PLAY STORE SUBMISSION

📦 Convert Live URL to Signed Google Play (.aab) Package

Google and Microsoft provide the official, free PWABuilder tool that packages this app directly into an Android App Bundle (.aab):

  1. Visit PWABuilder.com (Google & Microsoft official).
  2. Enter your live HTTPS URL from Step 2 and click Start.
  3. Click Package for Android (Google Play). It generates your signed .aab package and Digital Asset Links.
  4. Log in to your Google Play Console ($25 one-time registration).
  5. Create a new app listing, upload the generated .aab file, and click Submit for Review!

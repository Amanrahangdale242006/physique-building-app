# AuraSculpt AI — Vision Calorie Tracker & Precision Physique Architect

**AuraSculpt AI** is an intelligent, visual-first fitness and nutrition application that tracks meal calories from photos, recommends tailored daily exercises, and gamifies your physique transformation through an 8-Tier League Ranking System.

---

## 🏆 The 8-Tier League Ranking System

Your physique consistency is ranked across 8 competitive tiers:

| Tier | Rank Name | RP Range | Unlocked Perks & Privileges |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🥉 | **Bronze** | 0 – 200 RP | Baseline calorie & macronutrient calculation |
| 🥈 | **Silver** | 201 – 500 RP | Anabolic hydration & water tracking engine |
| 🥇 | **Gold** | 501 – 1,000 RP | Nutrient timing & bodyweight trajectory forecasting |
| 💠 | **Platinum** | 1,001 – 1,800 RP | Advanced progressive overload & RPE set tracker |
| 💎 | **Diamond** | 1,801 – 3,000 RP | Visual silhouette comparison & Leucine MPS audit |
| 🔥 | **Heroic** | 3,001 – 4,800 RP | Peak week carbohydrate cycling & thermogenic optimizer |
| 👑 | **Legend** | 4,801 – 7,000 RP | Unlimited VIP AI Sports Scientist coaching engine |
| 🔮 | **Grandmaster** | 7,000+ RP | Grandmaster Crest, Golden Trophy & Infinite Prestige |

### How to Earn League Rank Points (RP):
* 📸 **Click & Log Whole Day Meals**: Earn **+50 RP** per meal analyzed with AI Vision.
* 🥩 **Hit Daily Protein Target**: Earn **+100 RP** when daily muscle-building protein is reached.
* 🏋️‍♂️ **Complete Daily Recommended Routine**: Earn **+150 RP** upon finishing your targeted workout.
* 💧 **Achieve Anabolic Hydration**: Earn **+30 RP** upon hitting daily optimal water goals.

## 🏋️‍♂️ Targeted Exercise & Workout Protocol

Calibrated directly to your chosen physique blueprint (**Clean Hypertrophy**, **Fat Loss & Shred**, **Body Recomposition**, or **Maximum Strength & Bulk**):

* **Dynamic Routines**:
  * **Push Day A**: Incline DB Press, Standing OHP, Cable Flyes, DB Lateral Raises, Dual-Rope Tricep Extensions.
  * **Pull Day A**: Weighted Pull-Ups / Lat Pulldown, T-Bar Row, Seated Face Pulls, Incline Bicep Curls, Hammer Curls.
  * **Leg Day A**: Barbell Squats, Romanian Deadlifts (RDL), Bulgarian Split Squats, Hamstring Curls, Standing Calf Raises.
  * **Upper Strength**: Flat Barbell Bench, Bent-Over Rows, Arnold Press, Weighted Dips.
* **Interactive Set Tracking**: Check off each set (`Set 1 [✓]`, `Set 2 [✓]...`), record weight lifted in kg, and monitor RPE targets.
* **Smart Rest Timer**: One-tap 60s/75s countdown with progress bar to ensure optimal ATP-CP recovery between working sets.

## 📱 Mobile Phone Optimization

* **Native Camera Integration**: Tap "Snap / Pick Meal Photo" on your phone to open your smartphone's camera directly (`capture="environment"`).
* **Mobile Bottom Navigation Bar**: Fixed thumb-friendly navigation bar (Dashboard, Scan Meal, Workouts, Leagues, Coach) matching native iOS/Android fitness apps.
* **Responsive Touch Layout**: Optimized tap targets, card padding, and smooth single-column scrolling for all smartphone screen sizes.
